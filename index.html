Frequently Asked Questions

How much does lawn care cost in Watertown, NY?

Single mows typically $45–$75. Weekly plans start around $45/visit, bi-weekly $55+. Fertilization $59+/app, cleanups $129+. Final price depends on size, slope, and condition — free quote, no surprises.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. Mowing is pay-as-you-go with no long-term contract. Fertilization programs are seasonal (5 visits) but you can cancel anytime with notice.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes — commercial mowers, trimmers, edgers, blowers, and bagging if requested. We also handle gas, blades, and disposal.

What if it rains on my mow day?

We shift you to the next dry day that week and text you automatically. You’re never charged for a skipped visit.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully insured and locally operated in Watertown, NY. Proof of insurance available on request.

How fast can you start?

Usually within 3–5 days in season. Call (315) 555-0199 for same-week availability.
